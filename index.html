CONTACT

RULES

  • The sigil follows the user's movement.
  • Objects react when the sigil makes contact.
  • Contact pushes objects away from the sigil.
  • Contact creates a temporary cyan glow.

CONTROLS

MOVE Move the mouse to control the sigil.
HOLD Hold the mouse to increase sigil tension.

V1 REFLECTION

Contact now combines the visual sigil, mouse movement, physical reaction, and atmosphere into one system. The previous tests explored these ideas separately, while this version connects them through contact. The part that feels most successful is the direct relationship between the user's movement and the objects around the sigil.

The system becomes less clear when too many objects react at the same time, because the main interaction can become visually noisy. The restrained cyan glow helps keep the focus on the sigil and its immediate surroundings. In v2, I would tighten the contact response further by making the reaction more precise and localized around the point of contact.
