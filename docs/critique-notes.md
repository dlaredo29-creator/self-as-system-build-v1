WHAT WORKS

- The sigil is easy to recognize.
- Mouse movement clearly controls it.
- Contact produces an obvious response.


WHAT DOES NOT WORK

- Too many objects reacting at once becomes distracting.
- The trail can become difficult to see.
- Some reactions feel too subtle.


WHAT I CHANGED

- Reduced the contact distance.
- Reduced the trail intensity.
- Increased the contact response.
